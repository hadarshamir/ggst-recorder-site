GGST Recorder

A personal tool that records Guilty Gear -Strive- replays on its owner's own PC and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

GGST Recorder uses Google sign-in only to upload videos, set their thumbnails and titles, and organise them into playlists on the signed-in account's YouTube channel, and to update a Google Sheet owned by that same account.

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No Google user data is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
